About Our Church

Our church was established in the 1900s. It started with a few faithful people who gathered to worship God. Through God's grace and mercy, the church has grown and is now a vibrant family of believers.


We are committed to spreading the Gospel, strengthening families, developing young people, and serving our community through Christ-centered ministry.
